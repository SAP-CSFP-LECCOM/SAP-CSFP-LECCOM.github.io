7. DATE ALIGNMENT EXAMPLE — CALENDAR ROWS
================================================================================

The first date must appear under its actual weekday. Calendar layout may need
4, 5, or 6 rows depending on month/year.

| [MASS ASSIGNMENTS]   | [MASS]   | [MASS]   | [MASS]   | [MASS]   | [MASS]   | [SPECIAL] [6PM]      |
+----------------------+----------+----------+----------+----------+----------+----------------------+

Repeat rows for weeks 2–6 only when required. Do not shift dates into the
wrong weekday column. Keep leading/trailing dates blank where outside the month.

================================================================================
8. IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS FOR CODING AI
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE
Create a monthly Catholic Church Lectors and Commentators Mass schedule that
replicates the supplied Excel schedule and can be generated for any month/year.

PAGE / PRINT LAYOUT
- Landscape orientation.
- Fit the complete schedule to one printed page where feasible.
- Use thin black borders and compact, readable typography.
- Preserve the formal Excel-like appearance.
- Use merged cells for the header, announcement area, and liturgical footer
  where appropriate.
- Header includes parish/church logos, archdiocese, parish, guild, month/year.
- Announcement area is at the top-right.
- Footer includes Prepared By and Reviewed By.

CALENDAR
- Seven columns: Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday.
- Dynamically calculate weekday alignment for day 1 of selected month.
- Support 4, 5, or 6 calendar rows.
- Do not hardcode any month-specific date positions.
- Keep out-of-month cells blank.
- Render date number at the top of each date cell.
- Sunday: five mass assignments:
    6:30AM (K), 8:00AM (E), 10:00AM (E), 5:00PM (E), 6:30PM (E)
- Monday: default NO MASS, but allow manual mass configuration.
- Tuesday-Friday: one 6:00AM (K) mass each day.
- Saturday: separate 6:00AM (K) special-mass area and 6:00PM (E) regular-mass area.
- Saturday 6:00AM special mass is usually first Saturday only.

MASS ASSIGNMENT ROLE LABELS
- C  = Commentator
- L1 = Lector 1
- RP = Reader of Prayer
- L2 = Lector 2
- PF = Prayer of the Faithful

ANNOUNCEMENTS
- Monthly meeting date
- Mass time and meeting time
- Birthday celebrants
- Monthly reminders / dress code
- Special announcements

LITURGICAL DATA
- Feast days
- Liturgical celebrations
- Special church events
- Event date and description
- Populate based on selected month and year; do not hardcode sample events.

FOOTER
- Prepared By
- Reviewed By

IMPORTANT
This is a schedule layout, not a generic month calendar. Sunday and Saturday have
multiple mass areas. Preserve their separate assignments and formatting.
